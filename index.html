About Indian Railways

🏛️

History

Indian Railways was established in 1853 with the first train running between Bombay and Thane. Today it is one of the world's largest railway networks under single management.

🌐

Network

With over 67,000 km of track spanning the entire country, Indian Railways connects more than 7,000 stations and serves both urban centres and remote areas.

🚀

Modern Initiatives

Vande Bharat Express semi-high-speed trains, electrification drives, Kavach safety technology, and the upcoming bullet-train corridor between Mumbai and Ahmedabad are reshaping rail travel.

♻️

Green Railways

Indian Railways aims to achieve Net Zero Carbon Emission by 2030 through 100% electrification and large-scale adoption of renewable energy across its operations.
